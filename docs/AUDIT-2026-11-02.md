# Tick CPU audit (2026-11-02, independent, read-only)

Question: fleet-combat x8, 40 ships, ~26 ms tick, ~19 ms serial, but ~300 ms process CPU per tick. Is the parallel design wrong?

## Setup
- Machine: Ryzen 7 9800X3D, 8 cores / 16 threads. FastParallel: 7 workers (PhysicalCores - 1), priority Lowest.
- Build: mod-v11 + bench-mod3, `DOTNET_gcServer=0` (workstation, concurrent, Interactive).
- Traces: WPR CPU.verbose + GPU.light + DotNET.verbose, system-wide, 1 ms samples + CSwitch.
  - `Bench\out\trackA\audA`: 40 ships, window 2-31 s, 302 ticks.
  - `Bench\out\trackA\audB`: 100 ships, window 1-35 s, 345 ticks. WPR dropped 2.16M events, so GC windows in B are unusable.
- Analysis tools (scratchpad `audit\`):
  - `inspect3`: the Inspect tool, changed to mark samples inside GC SuspendEE..RestartEE and to keep unresolved module and leaf-IP frames.
  - `classify.ps1`: sorts samples into work / spin / lock / gc / kernel. A stackless sample takes the majority category of the same leaf IP among stacked samples.
  - `ticksin.ps1`: counts ticks inside the ETW window, using the phase-timing QPC.
- Contention from other processes (node/dotnet/go builds, logged by bench): A 49% (max 79%), B 36%, audC 13-34%, audE 13-15%. Traced runs are slower (A 10.4 ticks/s). Per-tick values below come from trace fractions scaled to the untraced CPU of 300-306 ms/tick (b15ab, audC, audE).
- Stack coverage: A 64%, B 57%. Bounds below use stacked-only vs IP-mapped samples.

## 1. Where the ~300 ms CPU per tick goes (40 ships)

| thread | work | spin | lock | GC | kernel | total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| main (sim + draw) | ~47 | ~1 | ~0.1 | ~2.5 (bench pause/tick) | ~3.5 | ~53 |
| 7 FastParallel workers | ~35 | **~193** (range 186-204) | ~0.3 | ~0.3 | ~2 | ~230 |
| other threads (audio, music decode, etc.) | ~15 | 0 | 0 | ~0.4 | ~2 | ~17 |

- Spin is 62-68% of all process CPU. All of it is one call site: `SpinWait.SpinOnce(-1)` in the outer loop of `FastParallel.RunThread` (`HalflingCore\Halfling.Performance\FastParallel.cs:633`). Its leaf IPs are the coreclr pause loop, `SwitchToThread` and `Sleep(0)`.
- Cause: `SimRoot.Update` (`Cosmoteer.Simulation\SimRoot.cs:830-838`) wraps the whole sim Update (fixed ticks, the per-frame sim Update and ExecuteQueued) in `FastParallel.Start()/Stop()`. While `IsRunning` is set, 7 workers spin without ever reaching `Sleep(1)`. They block on `s_wait` only during Draw. Codex adds: about 13 ms per tick of the spin falls outside fixed ticks and Draw (per-frame sim Update).
- Workers: about 15% of their CPU is task work and 85% is idle polling.
- Lock contention: Monitor/Lock/ReaderWriterLock frames total under 1 ms per tick. This is a sampled lower bound, not a ceiling.
- Kernel time: mostly yield syscalls from the spin.
- Scheduling: workers spend 3% of their time Ready (preempted) and main 1%. No sign of oversubscription by the game itself.
- 100 ships (B): same shape. Workers take 77% of samples, and 77% of that is spin. Untraced CPU is ~500-530 ms/tick (b13, audB).

**Proof by experiment (`audE`, 2 reps interleaved, contention 13-15%, `UNOFFICIAL_PATCH_IDLE_PARK=1` confirmed active in UnofficialPatch.log):**

| arm | CPU ms/tick | cores busy | ticks/s | frame p50 | frame p99 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| base | 306.2 / 306.5 | 5.5 | 17.94 | 50.5 | 130.0 |
| idle park | 112.2 / 112.6 | 2.1 | 18.75 (+4.3%) | 49.4 (-2.2%) | 125.8 (-3.4%) |

- Parking removes 194 ms/tick, which matches the trace estimate of 193 ms.
- Removing the spin also speeds the game up slightly: +4.3% ticks/s, ~1 ms per tick (SMT, cache or boost relief for the main thread).

**Correction to earlier findings.** Every earlier idle-park arm was a no-op: b13 and audC, both run with `BENCH_PHASE_TIMING=1`.
- UnofficialPatch.log shows "Parallel idle parking active", then about 20 s later "disabled: Foreign patch on FastParallel dependency".
- The cause is bench `FramePhaseTiming.cs`, which patches `FastParallel.For`.
- audC pk vs base: CPU 313.6/306.4 vs 310.6/307.3 ms per tick, i.e. no change.
- The TRACK-A-PROGRESS line "b13 pk: no gain" is therefore invalid.

## 2. Algorithmic waste
- **Raycasts are not brute force.**
  - They use a two-level BVH: the world broadphase `DynamicTree` of body proxies, then `body.FixtureTree` (`World.cs:270-305`).
  - Time is spent in the wrapper frames themselves (traversal plus inlined shape tests). Condition delegates take ~10%.
  - 53% of raycast samples come from `FixedWeapon.GetEnemyPartInSights` (`FixedWeapon.cs:236-291`): one full-range ray per fixed weapon per tick. Its `TargetSearchInterval` cooldown is skipped whenever `HasNearbyEnemy` is true, which is always in combat.
  - 40% comes from beams on the main thread (`BeamEmitter.DoSecondaryRaycast`, `GetHitInfo`).
  - Honoring the cooldown is a gameplay change: lockstep-consistent if every peer runs it, but not vanilla-identical.
- **Per-tick recomputation:** weapon `WantsToFire` runs every tick even while the weapon reloads (`Weapon.TryFireAsyncStep`). It feeds `IsToggleOn`, so it is not removable without behavior change.
- **Cosmetic work inside the sim Update on workers:** `MultiMediaEffectNode.EffectAnchor.Update`, `AudioEffect.Update` and `MediaEffect.GetViewScaledDistanceFactor` cost ~3-5 ms CPU/tick traced. This is per-frame, not tick-critical.
- **Ship-count scaling, 40 → 100 ships (2.5x):**
  - serial 19.2 → 22.2 ms (x1.16); parallel 7.5 → 12.4 ms (x1.65); CPU 300 → ~510 ms/tick.
  - No O(n^2) growth is visible, but the comparison is confounded: the 100-ship run is earlier in the battle (fewer beams), and contacts, fixtures, beams and splits were not counted.
- **GC:**
  - 1.9 MB/tick allocated (3.1 at 100 ships); ~65 GCs per 90 s, of which ~60 are gen1.
  - Pause is 2.45 ms/tick on average (4.9 at 100 ships), at 30-60 ms per GC. This is the spike source.
  - Top sampled allocators: music decode 40 MB, `VirtualSoundInstance.StopImpl` delegate removal, `RemoveAll`, `PartStateDictionary.Set`, `ReversibleMultiDictionary.Add`, `FixedWeapon.GetEnemyPartInSights` closures, subnetwork query cache events.
  - `DOTNET_GCgen0size=0x10000000` (audC, 2 reps): GCs 65 → 24, pause 2.45 → 1.55 ms/tick, frame p99 157-170 ms vs ~190 ms. Tick time unchanged within noise.

## 4. Ranked fix list
det-safe = does not touch sim state or order.

1. **Turn on worker parking by default; fix its guard.** det-safe (scheduling only).
   - Measured gain: -194 ms CPU/tick (-63%) and +4.3% ticks/s (~1 ms/tick); frame p99 -3.4%.
   - Smallest complete fix: keep `ParallelIdleParking` and give the bench `FramePhaseTiming` For hook an exact allowlist entry (owner, target, hook), so the guard stops disarming. Then flip the default after the det-x8/detci gate.
   - Codex: no notably smaller notification design. `Sleep(1)` via `SpinOnce()` is not equivalent (wake latency on every region).
2. **Cut serial main-thread work.** This is the main lever: removing all of the parallel phase would at most give -28% tick time, while serial is 72%. Not det-safe by default; every change needs det-x8 plus detci.
   - beams (~30%)
   - hit effects / ExecuteQueued (~16%)
   - World.Step (~15%)
   - ship split and SmoothedValue (~10%)
3. **Reduce allocations** (only mod-shippable GC lever): -0.9 ms/tick and fewer 30-60 ms spikes, judged by what gen0 sizing achieved.
   - Start with the sim-side top allocators (delegate removal, dictionary resizes, closures).
   - `GCgen0size` and server GC need env/runtimeconfig and cannot ship in a Workshop mod.
   - `GCSettings.LatencyMode=SustainedLowLatency` does not address gen1 pauses (Codex, MS docs).
4. **Worker count:** no oversubscription evidence. With parking on, re-test 7 vs fewer workers, which may still matter for SMT/boost (Codex). Expected gain 0-1 ms; det-safe (detci cores1/2 identical).
5. **Fixed-weapon LOS cooldown in combat** (`FixedWeapon.GetEnemyPartInSights`): about half of the FireWeaponsAsyncStep raycasts, roughly 2-3 ms CPU/tick in the parallel phase, under 1 ms wall. Gameplay change, so skip under the vanilla-identical rule.
6. **Deletion candidate (ponytail):** `UNOFFICIAL_PATCH_FP_THREADS` stays diagnostic only; no evidence for changing it. Do not add a spatial-index rewrite for raycasts: the BVH already exists.

## Codex second opinion (agent-link, 2026-11-02)
- **Agrees:** the pool burns CPU idle-polling; the old parking A/B is invalid; raycasts are a BVH and keeping the cooldown is the vanilla-identical choice; the GC experiment holds, but GC tuning is not shippable from the mod.
- **Disagrees:**
  - Serial is not the only lever (the parallel phase is up to 28% of tick time).
  - Per-tick spin numbers are estimates until measured. They now are: audE gives 194 ms.
  - Shared kernel and SpinWait leaf IPs are ambiguous, so they are reported as a range.
  - "No O(n^2)" cannot be concluded from two different battle phases.
  - Worker-count experiments should not be excluded.
- **Answers:** spin can hurt the main thread despite Lowest priority (SMT and shared resources), which audE confirms at about 1 ms/tick. Kernel CPU spent inside the idle loop counts as polling overhead.
